About

Mohamed Kanso

Fairdraft Studio is me, in Montpellier. Here is where I come from and how I work.

I've worked for myself since 2022, and with AI every day since 2023: I evaluate AI model outputs in training and evaluation projects. My job is to spot where an AI gets things wrong.

That experience is exactly what I bring here: I know what you can hand over to an AI, what needs guardrails, and what it's better not to ask of it. In my automations, AI never fills a gap on its own: anything missing is flagged, not invented.

Education: bachelor's in management, MSc in finance (Montpellier Business School), Master's in information systems (Université Grenoble Alpes). I speak English, French and Arabic.

Registered as a micro-entrepreneur in France since January 2025.
