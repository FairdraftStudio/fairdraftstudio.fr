About

Mohamed Kanso

Suivel is me, in Montpellier. Here is where I come from and how I work.

Three degrees, three languages. A bachelor's in management, an MSc in finance at Montpellier Business School, then a Master's in information systems at Université Grenoble Alpes. I speak English, French and Arabic.

Evaluating AI every day since 2023. Self-employed since 2022, I help train and evaluate AI models, working with different types of data. My job is to spot where an AI gets things wrong.

That experience shapes every automation. I know what you can hand over to an AI, what needs guardrails, and what it's better not to ask of it. In my automations, AI never fills a gap on its own: anything missing is flagged, not invented.

Today: automating your admin. With Suivel, I automate invoice reminders, meeting notes and the texts you rewrite every week, on your own tools and at a fixed price, from Montpellier and remotely anywhere in France.
