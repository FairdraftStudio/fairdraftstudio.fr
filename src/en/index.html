E-invoicing in France

E-invoicing is becoming mandatory in France

It covers invoices between businesses, including micro-businesses. A good moment to put your whole quote, invoice and reminder cycle in order.

  1. 1 Sept 2026

    Receiving

    Every business, whatever its size, must be able to receive invoices electronically.

    In force

  2. 1 Sept 2027

    Sending

    SMEs and micro-businesses will also have to send their invoices to other businesses electronically, through an approved platform (plateforme agréée).

    Coming up

Source: impots.gouv.fr (in French). Fairdraft Studio is not an approved platform and does not transmit your invoices. I set up your tools and reminders around the platform you choose.
