I'm not technical. Is this for me?

Yes. Nothing to install or code. During set-up you connect your own accounts yourself (a few minutes, with my guidance). After that, the automation runs on its own.

Are there costs on top of the price?

Usually, yes. Multi-step automations run on n8n, an automation tool, with a subscription in your name. Those that use AI, such as meeting notes, add a pay-per-use cost, and the AI assistant needs a Claude subscription. For your first automation, I pick a free option when one is enough. The cost is written on the quote and you pay the provider directly, with no mark-up from me.

Which tools does it work with?

Email: Gmail or Outlook. Spreadsheets: Google Sheets or Excel Online. Invoicing: your software if it allows it, otherwise a simple spreadsheet. I check your tools on a call or in writing, before any payment.

What about my data?

It stays in your tools, on accounts in your name. When a step uses AI, the text involved is sent to the chosen AI service (for example Claude, by Anthropic); the quote says so.

What if something stops working?

For 30 days after delivery, I fix any defect in what I delivered at no cost. After that, it's covered by monthly support at €49/month, or by a repair at a fixed price, stated before I start.

Are you an e-invoicing platform?

No. I'm not an approved platform and I don't issue invoices for you. I set up your tools around the platform you choose.

How does payment work?

By bank transfer, against an invoice: 50% when you order and the balance on delivery. For the €99 and €149 offers, the full amount when you order.
